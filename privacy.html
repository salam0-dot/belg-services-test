Privacy Policy

How we protect and handle your personal information

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account, including your name, email address, phone number, and profile information. For workers and companies, we may collect additional professional information such as skills, portfolio, and pricing.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to facilitate bookings, process payments, enable communication between users, improve our services, and comply with legal obligations.

Data Protection

Your personal data is protected using industry-standard encryption. Contact information for workers is only revealed to clients after payment confirmation to ensure safety for all parties.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as necessary to provide our services (e.g., between clients and workers for bookings) or when required by law.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You may also request a copy of your data or withdraw consent at any time.

Cookies

We use cookies to improve your browsing experience, remember your preferences, and analyze site traffic.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@belg-services.com
